Dyno

02ECOSYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

One intelligence. Two products.
Modular solutions.

The Dyno ecosystem is organized in layers: DM ObservaIT in governance, Dyno AI as the central intelligence, two products and specialized solutions on the same business core.

  1. GovernanceDM ObservaITCompany, brand and governance of the ecosystem.
  2. AICentral layerDyno AIInterprets, analyzes, orchestrates and executes safely, within the permissions of each product and company.
  3. B2C · people and familiesDynoAppPersonal assistance on WhatsApp, with Dyno and Dina.B2B · companiesDyno BusinessHorizontal and modular platform, for any industry.
  4. Solutions on the same Dyno Business core
    • Dyno SalesCRM, sales and sales automation.
    • Dyno FinanceFinancial management and billing.
    • Dyno FlowProcesses, tasks and approvals.
    • Dyno IntelligenceDashboards, indicators and AI-powered analysis.
    • Dyno ConnectIntegrations, APIs and connectors.
  5. Shared servicesIdentity · Permissions · Billing · Events · Audit · Notifications · Observability

Reference architecture. Sharing technology does not imply sharing data between products and companies, and not all capabilities are available today: the status of each solution is shown below.

03DYNO AI

The intelligence behind everything.

An AI layer that interprets, analyzes and automates in Dyno products, respecting data, modules and permissions. The mascots are the way to talk; Dyno AI is the engine.

  1. 01 / UNDERSTANDS

    Natural language

    Text, audio, photo or PDF become a clear request.

  2. 02 / CHECKS

    Authorized context

    User, company and permissions come from authenticated access, never from the message text.

  3. 03 / CONFIRMS

    Human control

    Sensitive actions go through review; without an enabled module, the action is denied.

  4. 04 / RECORDS

    Verifiable answers

    It states what it looked up and what it changed, with a record per product and company.
