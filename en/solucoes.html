02OBSERVABILITY

Monitoring shows what happened.
Observing helps you understand why.

Monitoring tracks what the team already knows to watch. Observability combines metrics, logs, traces and events to investigate how applications and infrastructure really behave, including in the face of questions no one anticipated.

  • Metrics

    Numbers over time: latency, error rate, CPU and memory usage. They show that something changed.

  • Logs

    The record of what each service did and when something failed. They help find the reason.

  • Traces

    The path of a request across services. They show where time is spent and where the chain breaks.

  • Events

    Changes and occurrences: deploys, alerts, configuration changes. They link the symptom to the change.

Where DM Observability can start

Starting points that match the risk, maturity and scope of each environment.

  1. Observability assessment

    We map services, signals, blind spots and dependencies to understand what can be seen today and what cannot.

  2. Dashboards per service

    Dashboards that show the health and behavior of the service, not just resource consumption.

  3. Alerts that call for action

    Criteria by service, impact and owner, to reduce noise and prioritize what matters.

  4. Instrumentation with OpenTelemetry

    Applications emitting metrics, logs and traces in an open format, without depending on a single vendor.

  5. SLIs and SLOs

    Reliability targets tied to what the user perceives, to decide what to fix first.

  6. AIOps

    AI to group alerts, summarize incidents and point out correlations, always reviewed by the team.

Already use any of these tools?

There is no need to switch. The work starts from what exists: we assess whether today's signals help recognize impact, guide investigation and support decisions.

Scope of work

DM Observability works on a project basis: assessment, implementation and improvements. It does not offer on-call or 24×7 operations.

OBSERVABILITY TOOLS

Each tool covers part of the problem.

Prometheus, Grafana, Zabbix, Elastic and OpenTelemetry come in according to the scenario. The choice starts from your environment, not from an imposed tool.

  • METRICS AND ALERTS

    Prometheus

    Collects metrics as time series and fires alerts based on rules. It is widely used in Kubernetes and container environments.

    • Collection and retention of service and infrastructure metrics
    • Alert rules by service and impact
  • VISUALIZATION AND ALERTS

    Grafana

    Brings together data from several sources, such as Prometheus, Elastic and databases, in dashboards so the team sees the whole picture in one place.

    • Dashboards by service, team or business area
    • A single view of different sources
  • INFRASTRUCTURE AND NETWORK

    Zabbix

    Monitors servers, network and devices through agents, SNMP and templates, with triggers and notifications. It usually makes sense in traditional or hybrid environments.

    • Templates and triggers for servers, network and services
    • Integration with Grafana and other sources, when it makes sense
  • LOGS, SEARCH AND ANALYSIS

    Elastic

    The Elastic stack (Elasticsearch and Kibana) indexes logs and other data for fast search and analysis, and also covers metrics and application monitoring.

    • Log collection and indexing pipelines
    • Queries and dashboards in Kibana
  • OPEN INSTRUMENTATION

    OpenTelemetry

    Open standard for generating metrics, logs and traces in applications, without tying the company to an observability vendor.

    • Application instrumentation and use of the Collector
    • Sending signals to the chosen tools

How the pieces connect

A reference design: what each layer does on the way from the application to the decision.

  1. OPENTELEMETRY

    Instrument

    Applications start emitting metrics, logs and traces in an open format.

  2. PROMETHEUS · ELASTIC · ZABBIX

    Collect and store

    Each tool stores the type of signal it is strongest at: metrics, logs, infrastructure.

  3. GRAFANA · KIBANA

    Correlate

    Signals are cross-referenced by service and time, linking symptom, cause and impact.

  4. ALERTS AND TEAM

    Act and learn

    Actionable alerts, incident response and review of what was learned.
